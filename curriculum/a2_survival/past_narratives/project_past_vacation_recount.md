### Introduction

In this project, you will combine all the skills mastered across the *Past Narratives & Daily Life* section into your first comprehensive A2 performance portfolio: **a dual written and spoken recount of a past vacation, relocation trip, or weekend journey in Canada**.

This project mirrors the dual demands of the **TCF Canada examination**:
1. **Written Production (*Expression écrite*)**: An **80 to 120-word email or blog post** recounting a past journey under simulated computer-based test conditions (**TCF-SO**, 15 minutes, no autocorrect). This exercises the *passé composé with avoir and être*, narrative chronology markers (*d'abord, ensuite, soudain, finalement*), and subject-participle agreements.
2. **Oral Production (*Expression orale*)**: A **2-minute recorded unscripted oral recount** responding to the classic examiner question: *« Racontez-moi un voyage ou un week-end mémorable que vous avez fait récemment »* (mirroring Speaking Task 1 and Task 2 personal narrative components).

Completing this project will provide tangible, verifiable evidence in your portfolio that your ability to narrate completed past events has stabilized at the official **CEFR A2 level (NCLC 4–5)**.

### Before you start

Before starting your writing and recording session, set up your physical and digital testing station:

- **Disable Autocorrect & Grammar Plugins**: Open a plain text editor with no spellchecker or auto-capitalization enabled. On the real computer-based TCF exam, candidates type into an unassisted text field.
- **Physical Keyboard Layout**: Ensure your keyboard allows you to type standard French accents (*é, è, ê, à, ç, '*) fluidly.
- **Timing Equipment**: Prepare a countdown timer set to **15 minutes** for the writing task, and an audio recorder (such as your smartphone's voice memo app or [Vocaroo](https://vocaroo.com/)) with a **2-minute timer** for the speaking task.
- **Review Prior Lessons**: Review auxiliary selection in [Passé Composé with Avoir](#/lessons/a2-survival-french/passe-compose-avoir), DR & MRS VANDERTRAMP and agreement rules in [Passé Composé with Être](#/lessons/a2-survival-french/passe-compose-etre), and chronology transitions in [Narrative Chronology Connectors](#/lessons/a2-survival-french/narrative-chronology-connectors).

### Assignment

<div class="lesson-content__panel" markdown="1">

1. **Step 1: Read the Official TCF Prompt**  
   Read the dual examination prompt below:

   > **Épreuve d'expression écrite & orale — Niveau A2**  
   > **Écrit** : Rédaction d'un message de 80 à 120 mots (Temps : 15 minutes)  
   > **Oral** : Récit spontané de 2 minutes (Temps de préparation : 1 minute)  
   > 
   > **Sujet** :  
   > *Vous revenez d'un voyage ou d'une fin de semaine passée dans une autre région (par exemple en Gaspésie, dans les Rocheuses canadiennes, à Québec ou dans un parc national). Vous écrivez à un ami ou collègue pour raconter votre séjour : votre départ, votre hébergement, les activités que vous avez faites, un imprévu ou un moment fort, et votre retour.*

2. **Step 2: Timed 15-Minute Writing Take (80–120 Words)**  
   Start your 15-minute timer and begin typing your text. You must satisfy these linguistic requirements:
   - Use at least **four different verbs in the passé composé with avoir** (*visité, mangé, vu, acheté*).
   - Use at least **two movement or reflexive verbs in the passé composé with être** with correct feminine/plural subject agreements (*suis parti(e), sommes arrivés, me suis reposé(e)*).
   - Incorporate at least **three chronological connectors** (*d'abord, ensuite, puis, soudain, tout à coup, finalement*).
   - Mention the duration using **pendant** (*pendant trois jours*).
   - Maintain the word count strictly between **80 and 120 words**.

3. **Step 3: Exact Word Count Verification**  
   Count your words according to official France Éducation international (FEI) rules (every word separated by a space or apostrophe counts as one word; e.g. *j'ai voyagé* = 3 words: *j'* + *ai* + *voyagé*).  
   - If you wrote fewer than 80 words, expand your recount with sensory details or weather conditions.
   - If you wrote more than 120 words, edit ruthlessly to respect the upper boundary.

4. **Step 4: Unscripted 2-Minute Oral Recording**  
   Take 1 minute to review your written text for inspiration, then **close your text editor completely**.  
   - Start your audio recording device.
   - Speak for **1 minute 45 seconds to 2 minutes** recounting your trip spontaneously. Do not read from your screen or notes!
   - Concentrate on clear vowel sounds, correct auxiliary pronunciation (*j'ai* /ʒe/, *je suis* /ʒə sɥi/), and smooth liaisons (*nous‿avons voyagé* /nu.z‿a.vɔ̃ vwa.ja.ʒe/, *ils‿ont‿aimé* /il.z‿ɔ̃.t‿e.me/).

#### Scoring Rubric

Evaluate both components using the official France Éducation international (FEI) 4-criteria matrix adapted for A2 performance (Total: 20 points):

| Criterion | 0–1 points (Below A2) | 2–3 points (Emerging A2) | 4–5 points (Mastered A2 Benchmark) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Réalisation de la tâche (Task Completion)** | Fewer than 60 words; fails to recount trip details or activities; oral recording under 60 seconds. | 60–79 words (word-count penalty); basic recount attempted but lacks either departure, incident, or return. | 80–120 words; complete chronological recount (departure, activities, accommodation, return); oral sustained 1m45–2m00. |
| **2. Cohérence et organisation (Cohesion)** | Disjointed list of sentences; relies solely on *et* or *et puis*; confusing chronology. | Uses 1–2 basic connectors (*ensuite, après*); chronology is understandable but repetitive. | Fluid narrative structure using diverse markers (*d'abord, ensuite, pendant que, soudain, finalement*); well-punctuated clauses. |
| **3. Lexique (Vocabulary)** | Vocabulary too restricted to describe travel; frequent English terms; severe spelling errors. | Adequate basic travel vocabulary (*hôtel, train, restaurant*); occasional repetitive adjectives. | Rich, accurate travel and vacation lexicon (*hébergement, paysage, sentier, spécialités régionales*); accurate spelling. |
| **4. Morphosyntaxe (Grammar & Agreement)** | Pervasive present tense; unable to form passé composé; missing auxiliary verbs. | Passé composé attempted with frequent auxiliary errors (*avoir* vs *être*); agreements missing. | Accurate auxiliary selection (*avoir* vs *être*); correct regular and irregular participles; subject agreements applied consistently. |

**Score Benchmark**:
- **0–9 points**: Below A2 (Needs reinforcement of passé composé mechanics)
- **10–14 points**: Solid A2 (NCLC 4 equivalent)
- **15–20 points**: Strong A2 / Threshold of B1 (NCLC 5 equivalent)
