### Introduction

In this project, you will execute a complete, unscripted simulation of the single most interactive component of the TCF examination: **Task 2 of the TCF Canada Speaking test (*Expression orale — Tâche 2: Exercice en interaction*)**.

In the official exam, the examiner hands you a short prompt containing a document (such as a classified rental ad, a sports club notice, or a job posting). You have exactly **2 minutes to prepare your questions**, followed by a **3.5-minute recorded interaction** with the examiner.

Practical advice for this task (study guidance, not official FEI rules):
- **Lead the conversation**: The examiner plays the landlord or agent and answers your questions. Do not count on them to keep the exchange going for you.
- **Use formal vouvoiement**: In a call to a landlord you do not know, *vous* is the appropriate register. Overly casual phrasing (*« C'est combien ? »*) makes your French sound less appropriate to the situation.
- **Prepare plenty of distinct, relevant questions** (aiming for 8 to 10 is a useful practice target): repeating the same question or falling silent early weakens your performance.

Completing this project will provide verifiable audio evidence in your portfolio that your oral questioning skills have stabilized at the **A2/B1 threshold**.

### Before you start

Before starting your roleplay simulation, set up your physical and digital testing station:

- **Timing Equipment**: Prepare two timers: a countdown timer set to **2 minutes (120 seconds)** for preparation, and a second timer set to **3 minutes 30 seconds (210 seconds)** for the recorded interaction.
- **Audio Recording Tool**: Open your smartphone's voice memo app or an online recorder like [Vocaroo](https://vocaroo.com/).
- **Blank Paper & Pen**: Prepare a blank sheet of scrap paper. You are allowed to take notes during the 2-minute prep window, but **you are not allowed to read full written sentences** during the interaction.
- **Review Prior Lessons**: Review classified ad vocabulary in [Apartment Features and Ads](#/lessons/a2-survival-french/apartment-features-and-ads), object pronoun shortcuts in [Object Pronouns Direct and Indirect](#/lessons/a2-survival-french/object-pronouns-direct-indirect), and formal questioning syntax in [Formal Questioning Techniques](#/lessons/a2-survival-french/formal-questioning-techniques).

2. **Step 2: Timed 2-Minute Preparation Take**  
   Start your 2-minute timer. On your scrap paper, sketch your **6-Point Question Generation Grid**:
   - *Lieu & Métro*: What is the nearest station?
   - *Loyer & Charges*: Exact monthly price? Is heating/electricity included?
   - *Pièces & Électros*: Size of the bedrooms? Is the fridge/stove included?
   - *Commodités*: Is there an elevator, a balcony, or a parking space?
   - *Règlement*: Are pets allowed? Is it non-smoking?
   - *Visite*: When can you meet to visit?

3. **Step 3: Recorded 3.5-Minute Unscripted Interaction**  
   Start your audio recording device and the 3.5-minute timer. Speak aloud as if conversing with Monsieur Tremblay on the telephone:
   - **Opening**: Greet politely and state why you are calling (*« Bonjour Monsieur Tremblay, je vous appelle au sujet de votre annonce pour le 4½ à louer... »*).
   - **Inquiries**: Ask your questions naturally. After each question, pause for 3–5 seconds and imagine the landlord's answer, then react appropriately (*« D'accord, très bien », « Je vois », « C'est parfait »*) before launching your next question.
   - **Question Variety**: You must include at least:
     - 2 questions using **complex noun inversion** (*Le loyer comprend-il... ?*, *L'immeuble dispose-t-il... ?*).
     - 2 questions using **polite conditional openers** (*Pourriez-vous me dire si... ?*, *J'aimerais savoir si... ?*).
     - 2 questions using **interrogative quel** (*Quel est le montant... ?*, *Quelles sont les conditions... ?*).
     - 1 question using an **object pronoun** (*Quand est-il possible de le visiter ?*).
   - **Closing**: Thank the landlord politely and confirm a visit appointment (*« Je vous remercie beaucoup pour ces renseignements. À samedi pour la visite ! »*).

#### Scoring Rubric

Evaluate your oral performance with this **unofficial self-assessment rubric** for TCF Speaking Task 2 practice (designed for this course, not an FEI grid; Total: 20 points):

| Criterion | 0–1 points (Below A2) | 2–3 points (Emerging A2) | 4–5 points (Mastered A2/B1 Benchmark) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Réalisation de la tâche (Task Execution)** | Fewer than 5 questions asked; runs out of ideas before 2 minutes; requires examiner prompting. | Asks 5–7 questions; covers basic topics (rent, rooms) but runs out of steam before 3.5 minutes. | Asks 8–10+ distinct, relevant questions; sustains interaction smoothly for the full 3 minutes 30 seconds. |
| **2. Vouvoiement & Interaction (Pragmatic Register)** | Slips into *tu*; overly casual phrasing (*« C'est combien ? »*); no greeting or closing formula. | Uses *vous* consistently, but interactions are mechanical with little reaction to imagined answers. | Flawless *vouvoiement*; natural conversational markers (*« Très bien, merci », « C'est une bonne nouvelle »*); professional closing. |
| **3. Variété syntaxique (Question Variety)** | Relies exclusively on rising intonation (*« C'est grand ? »*, *« Il y a un métro ? »*); no inversion. | Uses *est-ce que* and basic questions; attempts 1 polite opener; no complex inversion. | Sophisticated mix of complex noun inversion (*Le loyer comprend-il... ?*), indirect openers (*J'aimerais savoir si...*), and *quel* agreements. |
| **4. Prononciation & Aisance (Phonetics & Fluency)** | Pervasive hesitations prevent comprehension; English question intonation; mispronounces *quel / loyer*. | Understandable pronunciation; occasional hesitation while thinking of question stems; basic liaisons made. | Fluid delivery without prolonged silences; clear vowel sounds; correct euphonic liaisons (*comprend-il* /kɔ̃.pʁɑ̃.t‿il/, *est-elle* /ɛ.t‿ɛl/). |

**Score Conversion**:
- **0–9 points**: Below A2 (Needs reinforcement of question formulas)
- **10–14 points**: Solid A2
- **15–20 points**: Strong A2 / Solid B1

(These self-assessment bands are not TCF scores and have no NCLC equivalent; only an official TCF result can give you an NCLC level.)

### Submitting your work

A successful submission proves:

1. **Autonomous Conversation Leadership**: Leading the 3.5-minute telephone exchange without awkward pauses or running out of questions.
2. **Syntactic Versatility**: Using a balanced variety of question forms (complex noun inversion, indirect conditional openers, and *quel* agreements) rather than relying exclusively on rising intonation.
3. **Flawless Vouvoiement**: Sustaining professional formal address (*vous*) throughout the entire interaction.
