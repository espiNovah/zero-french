### About Zéro French

Zéro French is a free, open-source curriculum for adults who want to learn French well enough to pass the **TCF Canada** and earn at least **NCLC 7** in every skill for Canadian immigration — with a safety margin above the cut-off.

It follows the method that made [The Odin Project](https://www.theodinproject.com/) work for web developers:

- **One path, in order.** You don't have to work out what to study next.
- **Written lessons plus the best free resources.** Each lesson explains the idea, then points you to the best free material on the internet to go deeper.
- **Projects, constantly.** Every few lessons you complete a practice task that mirrors the real TCF Canada format — timed writing, recorded speaking, scored listening and reading — so the exam is never a surprise.

### Who it's for

Adult English speakers starting from zero, studying about 10 hours a week. If you already have some French, use the placement guide to find your starting path.

The main use cases:

- **Express Entry applicants:** need NCLC 7+ in French to maximise CRS points for permanent residency.
- **PNP candidates:** many Provincial Nominee Programs award extra points or require a minimum French level.
- **Citizenship applicants:** need to demonstrate adequate French (or English) proficiency for Canadian citizenship.
- **Complete beginners:** the A1 path starts from absolute zero — no prior French knowledge required.

### The paths

| Path | What you'll be able to do | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 Basics | Greet people, introduce yourself, order food, use basic grammar and everyday vocabulary | ~100 |
| A2 Survival French | Handle daily tasks: shopping, asking directions, making appointments, describing your routine | ~100 |
| B1 Independent French | Discuss familiar topics, narrate events, express opinions, read news articles and follow podcasts | ~200 |
| B2 Advanced French | Argue a position, follow complex discussions, write structured texts, understand nuance | ~200 |
| TCF Exam Prep | Master every TCF Canada task type with full timed mocks and score tracking | ~60 |
| B2+ Safety Margin (elective) | Push all four skills above the NCLC 7 cut-off to build confidence and absorb exam-day nerves | ~60 |

The full curriculum covers roughly 720 hours. At 10 hours a week, that's about 18 months. Treat the hours as guides, not deadlines.

### How to succeed

- **Go in order.** Each lesson assumes the ones before it.
- **Do every project.** Practice without production is how people plateau. If a project feels too hard, it's probably the right size.
- **Use spaced repetition.** Vocabulary and grammar stick when you review them at growing intervals.
- **Immerse yourself.** Listen to French every day — Canadian radio, podcasts, YouTube. Read French news. Even 15 minutes of daily input makes a measurable difference.
- **Record yourself speaking.** You can't improve what you don't measure. The speaking projects use the real TCF format so you get used to the time pressure.

### Your progress
