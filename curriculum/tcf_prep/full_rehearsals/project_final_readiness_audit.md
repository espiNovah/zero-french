### Introduction

Congratulations on reaching the final capstone project of **Path 04: TCF Canada Exam Prep** — the **Final Readiness Audit & NCLC Score Verification**.

Across the eighty-four lessons and projects spanning the Zéro French curriculum—from your initial phonetics drills in A1 Basics, through past narratives and conditional hypotheses in A2 and B1, to advanced argumentation, complex audio analysis, and high-pressure exam simulations in B2 and TCF Prep—you have built a formidable linguistic and tactical foundation.

Now, before you invest between **$350 and $450 CAD** in an official exam registration fee and schedule your test sitting, you must submit your entire preparation history to a **forensic, uninflated Readiness Audit**.

In Canadian economic immigration (Express Entry, Provincial Nominee Programs, Francophone Mobility Program), there is no partial credit for borderline performance:
- Reaching NCLC 7 in three competencies while slipping to NCLC 6 in a single competency **forfeits the entire 50-point Francophone bonus**.
- Hoping for "good luck on test day" is a statistically bankrupt strategy. Luck cannot compensate for a 15-minute pacing deficit in Reading, an unassisted typing speed below 20 WPM, or a 90-second freeze in the oral monologue.

The **Final Readiness Audit** is your formal "Go / No-Go" decision gate. You will assemble tangible evidence from your rehearsal exams, verify word counts and audio timestamps, compute your Safety Margin Index across all four competencies, and confirm that your readiness is grounded in verified psychometric data.

#### Part 1: Compréhension orale Verification (Target: $\ge$ 458 Scaled Points)

Audit your Listening performance from Rehearsal Sitting #2 against the official France Éducation international (FEI) benchmarks:

1. **Raw Score Threshold**: Did you score at least **28 out of 39 items correct** under single-exposure (*écoute unique*) conditions?
2. **Distractor Audit**: Review your error log:
   - Were your errors restricted to advanced C1/C2 items (Items 30 to 39)?
   - Did you achieve at least **14 out of 15** on Level A1–A2 items, and at least **9 out of 12** on Level B1 items?
3. **The Five-Second Drop Execution**: Confirm that when you encountered an ambiguous item, you dropped it within 5 seconds and did not allow it to disrupt your preview window for the next question.

---

#### Part 2: Compréhension écrite Verification (Target: $\ge$ 453 Scaled Points)

Audit your Reading performance from Rehearsal Sitting #2:

1. **Raw Score Threshold**: Did you score at least **28 out of 39 items correct**?
2. **Pacing Discipline**: Check your recorded timestamp log:
   - Checkpoint 1 (Item 15): Submitted with $\ge$ 45:00 remaining on screen.
   - Checkpoint 2 (Item 27): Submitted with $\ge$ 30:00 remaining on screen.
   - Preserved $\ge$ 25 dedicated minutes for Items 28 to 39.
3. **Zero-Blank Audit**: Confirm that all 39 questions were answered before the timer expired.

---

#### Part 3: Expression écrite Verification (Target: $\ge$ 12 / 20 Points)

Audit your three typed texts from the 60-Minute Writing Simulation:

1. **Word Count Compliance**:
   - Task 1: Exact count between **60 and 120 words** (optimal: 80–95 words).
   - Task 2: Exact count between **120 and 150 words** (optimal: 130–145 words).
   - Task 3: Exact count between **120 and 180 words** (optimal: 150–165 words).
2. **Structural Bipartite Balance in Task 2**:
   - Paragraph 1 (Narrative Recount): 60–65 words.
   - Paragraph 2 (Civic Stance): 70–75 words.
3. **Plagiarism Audit in Task 3**:
   - Confirm that **zero sequences of 3 or more consecutive words** were copied verbatim from Document 1 or Document 2.
   - Verify that nominalization and polarity inversion were used to paraphrase source arguments.

---

#### Part 4: Expression orale Verification (Target: $\ge$ 12 / 20 Points)

Audit your continuous 12-minute audio recording:

1. **Task 1 (Entretien dirigé — 2:00)**:
   - Four modules delivered clearly: Identity, Trajectory, Passions, Canadian Project.
   - Responded to the examiner's follow-up without long pauses or switching to English.
2. **Task 2 (Exercice en interaction — 5:30)**:
   - Two-minute silent preparation executed with scrap-paper shorthand (no full sentences).
   - Asked at least **10 distinct questions** using varied syntax (inversions, indirect clauses, *est-ce que*).
   - Overcame the examiner's deliberate objection with a constructive counter-proposal.
3. **Task 3 (Expression d'un point de vue — 4:30)**:
   - Spoke continuously for **at least 4 minutes and 15 seconds** without running out of ideas.
   - Deployed the "Rule of Three" (Economic, Human, and Societal lenses).
   - Anchored arguments in concrete Canadian or international examples.
   - Delivered a formal conclusion and future perspective before timer expiry.

#### The Final Readiness Decision Gate (Go / No-Go Protocol)

Before committing funds to book an official exam date, evaluate your readiness against the **Seven Non-Negotiable Criteria**:

| Criterion | Mandatory Standard | Your Status (PASS / FAIL) | Action if FAILED |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Listening Benchmark** | Rehearsal Sitting #2 scaled score $\ge$ 470 pts ($+12$ pt safety margin). | `[ PASS / FAIL ]` | Drill 3 full listening tests; enforce 5-second drop rule. |
| **2. Reading Benchmark** | Rehearsal Sitting #2 scaled score $\ge$ 465 pts ($+12$ pt safety margin). | `[ PASS / FAIL ]` | Drill Tier 1 & 2 speed skimming; enforce 15/15/30 clock. |
| **3. Writing Token Safety** | All 3 tasks strictly within official word boundaries in a single 60-min sitting. | `[ PASS / FAIL ]` | Complete two 50-minute compressed writing drills. |
| **4. Anti-Plagiarism Reflex**| Zero copied strings of 3+ words in Task 3. | `[ PASS / FAIL ]` | Drill the Nominalization Transformation Matrix. |
| **5. Oral Monologue Stamina**| Sustained monologue for $\ge$ 4:15 without stalling in Task 3. | `[ PASS / FAIL ]` | Practice 3 monologues using the Rule of Three grid. |
| **6. Unassisted Typing** | Raw typing speed in French $\ge$ 25 WPM without spellcheck or mouse palette. | `[ PASS / FAIL ]` | Practice daily 15-minute French typing drills on Keybr. |
| **7. Passport Verification** | Original, unexpired passport in hand matching registration character-for-character. | `[ PASS / FAIL ]` | Retrieve or renew passport before booking test date. |

**The Operational Verdict**:
- **7 out of 7 PASS**: **GO FOR OFFICIAL BOOKING**. You possess the linguistic competence, pacing automatisms, and psychological resilience to achieve NCLC 7 comfortably.
- **Any Single FAIL**: **NO-GO**. Address the specific failing criterion with a 5-day targeted sprint before committing test fees.
