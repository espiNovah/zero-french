### Introduction

Welcome to the capstone project of **Exam Mechanics & Distractor Dissection** in **Path 04: TCF Canada Exam Prep**.

In the previous four lessons of this module, you systematically deconstructed the psychometric architecture of the computer-delivered TCF Canada (*TCF-SO*):
1. **Listening Prediction and Choice Scanning**: You learned how to weaponize the 10-second preview window, conduct vertical semantic sweeps, and prime lexical fields before the audio begins.
2. **Distractor Dissection**: You mastered recognizing restrictive negations (*ne... que*, *guère*), false friends (*éventuellement*, *ignorer*), and chronological misdirection designed by test psychometricians to lure careless candidates.
3. **Reading Pacing Blueprint**: You internalized the strict 15 / 15 / 30 time budget, locking in the easy points of Levels A1–A2 in 15 minutes, clearing B1 in 15 minutes, and preserving 30 dedicated minutes for the complex multi-paragraph texts of B2 and C1.
4. **The Five-Second Drop Rule**: You acquired the emotional discipline and somatic protocols required to let go of missed audio questions within five seconds, halting catastrophic cognitive domino collapses.

Now, you must fuse these four mechanical skillsets under authentic, continuous test-center pressure.

The **Back-to-Back Timed MCQ Sprint** is a 95-minute endurance trial that combines the **35-minute Compréhension orale** (39 items) directly with the **60-minute Compréhension écrite** (39 items). In the official exam center, there is no coffee break, no pause button, and no second chance. Your ability to sustain sharp analytical focus across 78 consecutive multiple-choice questions determines whether you achieve the required **NCLC 7 threshold** (minimum 458 points in Listening, 453 points in Reading) or fall victim to late-session cognitive fatigue.

### Before you start

To produce an uninflated, audit-grade baseline of your receptive exam performance, set up your testing environment according to official France Éducation international (FEI) specifications:

- **Uninterrupted Testing Block**: Block out exactly **1 hour and 45 minutes** (10 minutes for setup, 35 minutes for listening, 60 minutes for reading). You must not pause the timer, check your phone, open dictionary tabs, or take a recess between sections.
- **Hardware & Peripherals**: Take this simulation on a desktop or laptop computer with a physical mouse or trackpad. Do not complete this on a smartphone screen; reading dense multi-column texts on a mobile screen distorts your visual scanning speed.
- **Audio Gear**: Use closed-back over-ear headphones or well-fitted in-ear monitors. In official test centers, ambient noise from adjacent keyboards, nervous clicking, and neighboring test-takers is constant.
- **Testing Material Sources**: Prepare an unattempted 39-question listening session and an unattempted 39-question reading session from verified official providers:
  - [TV5Monde — Simulateur d'entraînement au TCF](https://apprendre.tv5monde.com/fr) (official partnership with France Éducation international).
  - [France Éducation international — Exemples d'épreuves TCF](https://www.france-education-international.fr/).
- **Diagnostic Logging**: Have your diagnostic score tracker ready (or open a clean markdown document `tcf-mcq-sprint-log.md`) to record question-by-question timestamps, distractor classifications, and emotional drop ratings.

#### Part 1: Compréhension orale Simulation (35 Minutes, 39 Questions)

Launch your 39-question listening test. From the moment the first question appears on screen, execute the **Listening Mechanics Protocol**:

1. **Phase 1: Items 1 to 15 (A1–A2 Foundational Items — ~10 minutes)**:
   - Identify the communicative setting within the first two seconds (*gare, magasin, restaurant, bureau, message téléphonique*).
   - Beware of acoustic trap words: when the question asks about *le train pour Lyon*, do not click an option simply because you heard the word *Lyon* if the speaker said **« Le train en provenance de Lyon a dix minutes de retard »** — /lə tʁɛ̃ ɑ̃ pʁɔv.nɑ̃s də ljɔ̃ a di mi.nyt də ʁə.taʁ/ (*The train arriving from Lyon is ten minutes late*).
   - Confirm your choice within 3 seconds of audio completion and lock your attention onto the next item immediately.

2. **Phase 2: Items 16 to 27 (B1 Intermediate Items — ~12 minutes)**:
   - Apply the **Ten-Second Eye Sweep**: identify the grammatical subject and main verb of all four choices before the audio starts.
   - Watch for explicit negations and nuance markers: **« Ce n'est pas tout à fait ce qui était convenu »** — /sə nɛ pa tu.t‿a fɛ sə ki e.tɛ kɔ̃v.ny/ (*That is not quite what was agreed upon*).
   - If an answer is not obvious after listening, eliminate the two most blatant distractors, make an educated guess between the remaining two, and move forward.

3. **Phase 3: Items 28 to 39 (B2–C1 Advanced Items — ~13 minutes)**:
   - Deploy **Active Ideational Tracking**: do not transcribe words mentally; map the logical stance of the speakers (*adhésion, scepticisme, désapprobation polie, résignation*).
   - **Enforce the Five-Second Drop Rule**: If you lose the thread of a fast Canadian radio debate (*Radio-Canada*) or an academic interview, you have exactly **five seconds** after the tone to click a default option (e.g., choice B or C) and force your eyes to the next item's preview window. Never permit a missed question on item 31 to destroy your preview window for item 32.

#### Part 2: Compréhension écrite Simulation (60 Minutes, 39 Questions)

The moment the listening test ends, your 60-minute reading timer begins. Immediately activate the **15 / 15 / 30 Pacing Blueprint**:

1. **Tier 1: Items 1 to 15 (A1–A2 Short Documents — Budget: 15 Minutes)**:
   - Target documents: SMS messages, brief emails, store opening hours, public transport flyers, classified ads.
   - Target pace: **1 minute per item**.
   - **Checkpoint 1 (Screen Clock reads 45:00 remaining)**: You must have submitted your answer for item 15. If your clock shows 43:00, you are behind schedule. Accelerate immediately by eliminating decorative options and clicking the direct factual match.

2. **Tier 2: Items 16 to 27 (B1 Practical Texts & Articles — Budget: 15 Minutes)**:
   - Target documents: Job descriptions, administrative notices, consumer advice articles, cultural event reviews.
   - Target pace: **1 minute 15 seconds per item**.
   - Technique: Read the question prompt first, identify key operational nouns (*motif, délai, public cible, exigence*), scan the text for synonymous lexical fields, and cross-verify.
   - **Checkpoint 2 (Screen Clock reads 30:00 remaining)**: You must have answered item 27. You have now banked exactly **30 full minutes** for the 12 most difficult questions on the examination.

3. **Tier 3: Items 28 to 39 (B2–C1 Journalistic Editorials & Research Syntheses — Budget: 30 Minutes)**:
   - Target documents: Complex journalistic opinion pieces (*Le Devoir*, *Le Monde*), sociological analyses, environmental reports, literary commentary.
   - Target pace: **2 minutes 30 seconds per item**.
   - Watch for high-level distractors:
     - **The Absolute Trap**: Options containing *toujours, jamais, exclusivement, totalement* when the author uses cautious modalization (*semble indiquer, tend à prouver*).
     - **The Literal Lexical Echo**: Options repeating the author's exact poetic phrasing to assert an interpretation opposite to the text.
     - **The False Friend Trap**: Watch out for words like *actuellement* (meaning *currently*, not *actually*) and *éventuellement* (meaning *potentially*, not *eventually*).
   - **Checkpoint 3 (Screen Clock reads 10:00 remaining)**: You must reach item 35.
   - **Checkpoint 4 (Screen Clock reads 02:00 remaining)**: Every single item from 1 to 39 must be answered. Verify that zero items are left blank. Since the TCF Canada carries **no negative marking**, an unanswered question is an automatic zero.

#### Extra credit

Candidates aiming for an impenetrable C1 safety margin (NCLC 8 or 9) can complete two high-intensity extension drills:

1. **The Compressed Reading Drill (45-Minute Limit)**: Take a fresh 39-question reading test with an accelerated countdown timer set to **45 minutes** instead of 60 minutes. Reaching 28/39 under a 45-minute limit ensures that normal 60-minute test conditions feel spacious, calm, and immune to anxiety.
2. **The Transcript Reconstruction Audit**: Take the three most difficult listening audio items you missed during the sprint. Listen to each audio snippet three times and transcribe every spoken word by hand in French. Compare your transcription with the official transcript to identify exactly which spoken reduction (such as **« ch'sais pas »** for *je ne sais pas*, or **« y a »** for *il y a*) or Canadian phonetic feature caused the comprehension blackout.
